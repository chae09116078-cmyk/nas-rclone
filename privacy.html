Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

NAS-rclone is a private application used solely by the application owner for backing up personal NAS files to Google Drive.

Google User Data

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of uploading, reading, managing, and restoring files selected by the application owner.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens are stored on the application owner's private NAS. They are not shared with third parties.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Data Retention

Files stored in Google Drive remain there until the application owner removes them. OAuth access may be revoked by the owner at any time through their Google Account.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the email address listed as the developer contact in the Google OAuth configuration.
